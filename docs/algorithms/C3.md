# C3. Execution engine, including the feasible critical path

Increment: I2 (engine, plain critical path), I4 (feasible). One page when the function is finished.

## Two levels

- **Level 1 (known algorithm, our transformation):** Event-driven DAG scheduler: four join rules (all_success, any_success, all_done, at_least k), bounded parallelism, cancellation, goals.
- **Level 2 (built on the output of level 1):** Feasible critical path: the longest chain of steps whose optimistic-run conditions are jointly satisfiable (C2 and C6); priority of the ready queue.
- **Dependence:** Level 2 ranks ready steps by a chain that level 1 can actually execute; I2 ships the plain longest path as a baseline.
- **Baseline for the benchmark:** FIFO, plain longest path and feasible path: makespan on generated DAGs.
